## Phones and tablets

Cameras, displays, instruments and dedicated appliances made from spare mobile devices\.

- **[AlfredCamera](https://alfred.camera/)** — Assign a retired phone camera duty and view its feed from a newer device\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://support.alfred.camera/hc/en-us/articles/360039719851-How-do-I-set-up-AlfredCamera) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#alfredcamera).
- **[AudioRelay](https://audiorelay.net/downloads)** — Put an old Android phone beside you as a wireless speaker for computer audio\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://audiorelay.net/docs/windows/stream-audio-from-your-pc-to-your-phone) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#audiorelay).
- **[DroidCam](https://droidcam.app/)** — Reuse a spare phone as a camera and microphone for desktop calls or OBS\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://droidcam.app/help/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#droidcam).
- **[Gretel](https://github.com/abeant/gretel)** — Make a spare Android device return to one chosen app whenever Home is pressed\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/abeant/gretel) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#gretel).
- **[Mixbus TouchOSC Layout](https://github.com/nicbeu/Mixbus-TouchOSC-Layout)** — Use an older tablet as tactile mixer, transport, and channel controls for a recording workstation\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/nicbeu/Mixbus-TouchOSC-Layout) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#mixbus-touchosc).
- **[NavigatorOSM](https://github.com/vibe-maribit/NavigatorOSM)** — Give an early iPad a dedicated map and navigation interface\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://vibe-maribit.github.io/NavigatorOSM/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#navigatorosm).
- **[Octo4a](https://github.com/feelfreelinux/octo4a)** — Give an Android phone the job of hosting OctoPrint and watching a USB 3D printer\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/feelfreelinux/octo4a/wiki/FAQ) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#octo4a).
- **[OpenPhotoFrame](https://github.com/micw/OpenPhotoFrame)** — Turn an old Android tablet into a slideshow using local photos or a Nextcloud share\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/micw/OpenPhotoFrame/releases/tag/v1.12.1) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#openphotoframe).
- **[phyphox](https://phyphox.org/)** — Use a spare phone’s existing sensors for classroom measurements and export the resulting data\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/phyphox/id1127319693) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#phyphox).
- **[spacedesk](https://www.spacedesk.net/)** — Extend a Windows desktop onto an otherwise idle tablet\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://manual.spacedesk.net/SystemRequirements1.html) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#spacedesk).
- **[SuperDisplay](https://superdisplay.app/)** — Turn an Android tablet with a pressure\-sensitive stylus into a drawing surface for desktop art software\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://superdisplay.app/help/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#superdisplay).

## E\-readers

E\-ink dashboards, desktop companions and expanded reading workflows\.

- **[Desk Buddy](https://github.com/deskbuddy-app/desk-buddy)** — A 2013 Paperwhite becomes a desk display for tasks, calendar, music controls, and app shortcuts\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/deskbuddy-app/desk-buddy) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#desk-buddy).
- **[Kindle Carousel](https://github.com/qadzek/kindle-carousel)** — Use Kindle page buttons to browse a rotating set of e\-ink web dashboards\. *Source / DIY build* · [Evidence](https://github.com/qadzek/kindle-carousel) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#kindle-carousel).
- **[KOReader](https://github.com/koreader/koreader)** — Give supported e\-readers broader document formats, PDF reflow, dictionaries, and self\-hosted library access\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/koreader/koreader) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#koreader).

## Computers

Give a retired computer one clear job: entertainment, storage, automation or offline knowledge\.

- **[Batocera](https://batocera.org/)** — Boot a retired PC directly into a controller\-friendly retro gaming system\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://wiki.batocera.org/install_batocera) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#batocera).
- **[Daphile](https://www.daphile.com/)** — Run a headless music player and CD\-ripping station on a retired PC\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://www.daphile.com/download/DaphileInstallation.pdf) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#daphile).
- **[Home Assistant OS](https://www.home-assistant.io/installation/generic-x86-64/)** — Turn a spare mini PC into a dedicated home\-automation controller\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://www.home-assistant.io/installation/generic-x86-64/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#home-assistant-os).
- **[Kiwix Server](https://get.kiwix.org/en/solutions/applications/kiwix-server/)** — Let an old computer serve offline Wikipedia and other archived websites to nearby browsers\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://get.kiwix.org/en/solutions/applications/kiwix-server/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#kiwix-server).
- **[Kolibri](https://learningequality.org/kolibri/)** — Reuse modest computers as a local learning server and classroom terminals, with downloaded course content\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://kolibri.readthedocs.io/en/latest/install/system_requirements.html) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#kolibri).
- **[LibreELEC](https://libreelec.tv/downloads/generic/)** — Make an unused PC boot straight into Kodi for local and network media playback\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://wiki.libreelec.tv/hardware/intel-x86-64-generic) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#libreelec).
- **[openmediavault](https://www.openmediavault.org/)** — Give an old computer a shared\-storage role with browser\-managed folders and network file services\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://docs.openmediavault.org/en/latest/prerequisites.html) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#openmediavault).

## Retired connected devices

Keep useful hardware alive through replacement software, local services and new interfaces\.

- **[AirConnect](https://github.com/philippe44/AirConnect)** — Add an AirPlay audio entry point to older Sonos, UPnP, or Chromecast speakers\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/philippe44/AirConnect) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#airconnect).
- **[Gadgetbridge for Pebble](https://gadgetbridge.org/gadgets/wearables/pebble/)** — Keep an original Pebble useful through an independent Android companion and local watch management\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://gadgetbridge.org/basics/pairing/pebble/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#gadgetbridge-pebble).
- **[Lyrion Music Server](https://lyrion.org/getting-started/)** — Reconnect a Squeezebox to a local music library served by community\-maintained software\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://preview.lyrion.org/players-and-controllers/squeezebox-touch/) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#lyrion-squeezebox).
- **[MIRROR Repurpose](https://github.com/TimStewartJ/lululemon-mirror-repurpose)** — Replace the workout interface with a local clock, photo display, dashboard, or media receiver\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/TimStewartJ/lululemon-mirror-repurpose) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#mirror-repurpose).
- **[unmetaportal](https://github.com/fcjr/unmetaportal)** — Replace the retired Portal experience with an ordinary Android launcher and chosen applications\. *Available setup* · [Evidence](https://github.com/fcjr/unmetaportal) · [Setup & limits](docs/catalog.md#unmetaportal).
